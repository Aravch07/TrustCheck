How the score works

25 points · Review burst At least 5 other reviews of the business within ±1 hour.

25 points · Similar wording At least 2 other reviews have ≥70% shared unique words.

20 points · Rating surge Mean within ±1 day exceeds the earlier mean by >1 star; minimum 5 reviews in each group.

20 points · Repeat reviewer One account reviews the same business at least 3 times.

10 points · Rating mismatch Rating differs from the earlier mean by ≥2 stars; minimum 5 earlier reviews.

High risk ≥60 · Watchlist 30–59 · Low risk <30. Scores are calculated on the server from current records. A score indicates suspicion, not proof of fraud.
