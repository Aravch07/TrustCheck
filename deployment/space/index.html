Training and evaluation

Labels come from the simulator's known campaign membership—not the old rule score. Training, validation and test contain separate businesses and separate reviewer accounts. The threshold is selected on validation data, then evaluated once on the untouched test set.

Common synthetic phrase templates can occur in multiple splits. Scores may miss early campaign reviews and can falsely flag genuine bursts. The real-world performance of this model is unknown.
